Three steps, no sign-up.

  1. Choose your photos

    One photo or a whole batch, as JPEG, PNG or WebP, from your phone or computer. They open in your browser and go nowhere else.

  2. Say where and when

    Type an address, paste a Google Maps link, drop a pin or use your location. Set any date and time.

  3. Download it stamped

    Pick a map style, then save full-resolution photos as a ZIP or as one PDF, with the stamp drawn on and the location written into each file.

About Chumi Geo Tag

What it is

Chumi Geo Tag is a free tool that puts a place, an address and a time on a photo. Choose one picture or a whole batch, say where and when, and download them with a clean stamp and a real geotag inside each file, or as a PDF.

Your photo stays with you

The photo is read and drawn on in your browser. It is never uploaded. Only the address you search for is sent to OpenStreetMap's search service, and map pictures are fetched from OpenStreetMap and Esri.

Open source

The code is free to read, copy and improve under the MIT licence. View it on GitHub.

Please use it honestly

Chumi Geo Tag writes the place and time you choose onto a photo. It does not prove where or when a photo was taken, and you are responsible for how you use the result.
